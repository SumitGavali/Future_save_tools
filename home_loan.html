What is this tool about , and what does this charts says .

Designed specifically for Indian home loan borrowers who want to understand how making extra prepayments can significantly reduce their loan tenure and interest burden .



What the Graph Means

Stacked Bars Represent Each Month's Payment Breakdown
For every month, the graph shows how much of your payment goes towards the loan amount and how much goes toward interest.

Two Color-Coded Stacks:

    Blue Shade: original loan repayment amounts
    Green Shade: new loan repayment after prepayment adjustments.

Visual Comparison:

See how prepayments reduce interest components and escalate principal payments, leading to faster loan closure.

    X-Axis: Monthly installments (months).
    Y-Axis: Payment amount in rupees (₹).
